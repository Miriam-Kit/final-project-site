This site contains the design process of my “Rebranding Identity Project” for my DVC 1 class.

The project contents include drafting stages, research, and images of every step it took to get to the final product.

About

Project Purpose

This project is a rebrand for a selected local Milwaukee business, shifting its current archetype to a more aligned, strategic archetype. Students will develop a fresh visual identity that reflects the new archetype and core values, with a focus on modernizing the brand’s image while remaining true to its mission.

The business that I was given for this project was Boswell Book Company. Boswell is a bookstore with a wide variety of books, magazines, records and other items. The original archetype of the store was the sage which focuses on education and knowledge. The one I have rebranded it to is the lover which focuses on connection and passion.

Objectives

Students will select a Milwaukee-based business and assign it a new archetype, creating a comprehensive brand identity kit. Deliverables will include logos, color palette, typography, and applications across different platforms, culminating in a cohesive brand system.
